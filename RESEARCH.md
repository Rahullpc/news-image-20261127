## Lead story — the storm that stopped Thanksgiving

A powerful nor'easter swept the US East Coast on Thanksgiving Day,
and Macy's postponed its 100th Thanksgiving Day Parade in New York —
the first weather postponement in the parade's century of history.
The decision was announced on Wednesday afternoon, hours after crews
had finished inflating the giant character balloons. The National
Weather Service issued a high wind warning and a coastal flood
warning for New York City: sustained winds of 30–40 mph with gusts
to 60–65 mph, moderate to heavy rain and possible flash flooding
from Thanksgiving morning into Friday. Balloons must be grounded
when gusts pass 34 mph. The parade moves to Saturday 28 November
at 8:30 a.m. ET. The parade was suspended only in 1942–1944, during
the Second World War — never before for weather.

## Grid stories

### Trade — partners hit back at Trump's new tariffs

China, Japan and other trading partners protested President Trump's
new tariff plan, announced on Wednesday after an eight-month
Section 301 probe into excess industrial capacity. China warned it
could raise its tariffs to 40% if talks fail; the plan names 14
partners, with the EU facing 15–20%. The US Trade Representative
will not hold a hearing on the proposals until 25 January, so the
duties would take effect early next year at the earliest. Markets
took the delay as relief: European stock futures rose 1.4–2.2% and
Asian shares climbed while Wall Street stayed shut for Thanksgiving.

### Korea — Kim orders more tactical nuclear weapons

North Korea's Kim Jong Un inspected nuclear-material production and
weapons research facilities and ordered a wider deployment of
tactical nuclear weapons across multiple domains, state media KCNA
reported on Thursday. Kim said nuclear-material production capacity
was being systematically expanded and declared North Korea's status
as a nuclear weapons state irreversible and not up for negotiation.

### Egypt — 31 killed as fuel tanker fire engulfs Cairo highway

A tanker truck carrying petroleum products overturned on Cairo's
Ring Road in Giza on Wednesday evening, igniting a fire that killed
at least 31 people and injured 21 others, Egyptian authorities
said. The blaze above Tunnel 10 in the Warraq district burned at
least 27 vehicles; 39 ambulances were sent and prosecutors detained
a driver who confessed to speeding.

### Sport — Plzen thrash Benfica 4–0; Milan's comeback

Europa League Matchday 5, Thursday 26 November: Viktoria Plzen
thrashed previously unbeaten Benfica 4–0, scoring three goals in
the last 15 minutes of the first half. AC Milan came from 2–0 down
to win 4–2 at Olympiacos — Christian Pulisic's 71st-minute volley
put them ahead. Bournemouth kept their perfect record with a 2–1
comeback win at Celta Vigo.

### Sport — Bears stun Lions in Thanksgiving thriller

Caleb Williams threw a 6-yard touchdown pass to Rome Odunze with
three seconds left as the Chicago Bears beat the Detroit Lions
39–35 on Thanksgiving, their first lead of the game. The Bears
(9–2) took sole possession of first place in the NFC North; the
Lions fell to 8–3. Jahmyr Gibbs ran for 205 yards and three
touchdowns for Detroit in defeat.

### Markets — Nvidia put on CreditWatch

S&P Global put Nvidia on CreditWatch negative, a possible
one-notch cut within 90 days, while Moody's kept its Aa1 rating
stable. Nvidia shares fell 3.9% on Wednesday to $149.30, as
scrutiny of chief executive Jensen Huang's reinstatement kept
growing in Washington.
